Retesting

Your current maxes

Weeks 23, 47 and 52 are built-in 1-rep max tests, flagged on the day itself, so update these numbers straight after each one. That's what actually drives the year's strength gains: a moderate lifter training consistently at this intensity and retesting properly can expect to add somewhere in the region of 15-25% to these lifts by the final test, well above where they started. Every session that isn't logged yet recalculates its target weight from these numbers immediately.
